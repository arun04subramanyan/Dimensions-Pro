Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dimensions Pro ("the Extension") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Chrome extension.

1. Overview

Dimensions Pro is a browser extension designed to help designers and developers inspect UI dimensions, spacing, alignment, and layout consistency directly on webpages.

2. Information We Collect

Dimensions Pro does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information.

The extension does not collect:

  • Name or email address
  • Passwords or login credentials
  • Financial information
  • Health information
  • Location information
  • Browsing history
  • Personal communications

3. Website Access

When you activate the extension, it temporarily reads the content of the webpage you choose to inspect. This access is used solely to analyze UI elements, dimensions, spacing, and layout consistency.

All analysis is performed locally within your browser. No webpage data is transmitted to our servers.

4. Local Storage

The extension may store the following information locally on your device:

  • User preferences
  • Extension settings
  • Imported audit configuration files
  • Recently used options

This information remains on your device and is never shared with us.

5. Third-Party Services

Dimensions Pro does not use advertising services, analytics platforms, or tracking technologies.

If you choose to load an audit configuration file from an external source, only that requested resource is accessed.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party.

7. Data Security

Dimensions Pro is designed to process webpage information locally within your browser. We take reasonable measures to protect user privacy and do not retain personal data.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
